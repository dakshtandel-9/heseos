# BLDC Fans media

All hero media is generated and illustrative. Each film is re-encoded with H.264, a keyframe every 4–6 frames and no B-frames, so scroll-scrubbing seeks smoothly. Each poster is the film's first frame.

- `room.mp4`, `room-720.mp4`, `room-poster.webp`: the view from the fan, looking down at a living room where a man naps under a newspaper and a dupatta hangs over a chair. Keyframe: Higgsfield GPT Image 2.6 (high, 2K, 1:1). Film: Kling 3.0 Pro, 6 s, 1:1, image-to-video (the breeze builds and the fabric moves; the camera stays still, and the rotation is done in CSS).
- `film.mp4`, `film-720.mp4`, `film-poster.webp`: looking up at the Elegance Gold Black Marble fan as it spins up. Keyframe: GPT Image 2.6 (high, 2K, 16:9), with `fan-gold-black-marble.webp` as the reference. Film: Kling 3.0 Pro, 6 s, 16:9.
- `fan-*.webp`: cut-outs of the catalogue product renders.
- `ceiling.webp`: the old hero background. No longer used.
