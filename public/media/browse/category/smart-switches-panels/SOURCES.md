Regenerated 2026-11-27 to match the real panel (the earlier version used a near-square face with the mark top left).

Exploded: an edit of the turned frame, then an edit widening the rear layers (glass, metal edge, touch/LED board with pads behind the glass keys, control board, back housing).
  - Film: two Kling 3.0 std clips, 5 s each, sound off: front → turned, and turned → exploded (start and end frames), joined on the shared turned frame (241 frames at 24 fps). Kling dropped the top-left glass ring for frames 21–37 of the explode clip; it is restored locally by copying the bottom-left ring to the tracked position (lighten blend).
  - Encoded for scrubbing like the meter film: H.264, a keyframe every 4 frames, no B-frames, 1080p (upscaled from Kling's 720p) at CRF 22 and 720p at CRF 24, faststart, no audio. The poster is the first frame and the still is the last; pin positions in `stories.ts` are measured on the still.
