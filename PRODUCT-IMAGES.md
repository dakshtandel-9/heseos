# Product page images

Every product detail page has wide image slots. Until an image exists, its slot shows as a plain grey frame at the right aspect ratio.
Save a generated image at the path below and it appears on the page. No code change or restart is needed.

| Slot | Shape | Size | Where it shows | What to show |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| hero.webp | 21:9 wide | 2400×1030 | Overview, full width | The product installed in a premium, warmly lit modern Indian home, at true scale |
| feature-N.webp | 16:9 wide | 1920×1080 | Highlights row, one per feature | The feature in use: the product in a real room, showing what the feature does |
| detail.webp | 4:5 portrait | 1200×1500 | Take a closer look, left | Macro close-up of the finish, edges and materials in soft raking light |
| in-use.webp | 4:3 | 1600×1200 | Take a closer look, right | A hand or person using the product in an everyday moment at home |

Tips: keep the product accurate to the studio shot in public/media/products/studio/ (pass it as a reference image), use warm natural light, and keep text and logos out of the scene. Export as WebP at about 80% quality.
